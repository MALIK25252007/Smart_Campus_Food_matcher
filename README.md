### 🌱 The Problem

Every day, hostels and PGs around campus throw away food that's still good — while other students, often on tighter budgets, go without a proper meal. There's no simple system connecting the two. That gap is what this project set out to solve.

> This idea started in 2025 after seeing the food-availability problem among students firsthand, then grew through direct conversations with hostels and PGs in Greater Noida to understand the problem at ground level.

---

### 💡 The Idea

**Smart Campus Food Matcher** aims to connect surplus food from hostels/mess kitchens with students who need it — with a **donation leaderboard** to recognize contributing hostels, and a **claim system** so a student can claim a donated meal once a day.

| Problem | This Project's Approach |
|---|---|
| Surplus hostel food goes to waste | A donation flow for hostels/mess kitchens to list surplus food |
| No visibility into who's contributing | A public leaderboard ranking donor hostels |
| Students in need have no easy access point | A claim-once-a-day system, gated behind login |
